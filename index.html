How it started"xPerception started as a research question inside the lab, not a product brief: what is accurate 6D object pose estimation actually worth on a real production line? Chasing that question through years of 3D vision and robot-learning research, and the award-winning FreeZe algorithm it produced, turned into a technology validated on industrial hardware, and then into a company." Where it is going"Our ambition doesn't stop at pose estimation. We want xPerception to become the perceptual engine behind scalable, plug-and-play robotic automation, so that high-mix, low-volume manufacturing can run as efficiently as mass production always has."
